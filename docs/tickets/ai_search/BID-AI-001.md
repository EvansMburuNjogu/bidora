# BID-AI-001 — Approve AI privacy, budgets, evaluation corpus and missing job contracts

## Metadata
- Ticket ID: BID-AI-001
- Epic: E-AI
- Type: Design
- Priority: P0
- Status: Blocked
- Complexity: M
- Dependencies: BID-ARC-010, BID-ARC-008
- Blockers: OpenAI deployment/privacy acceptance, local embedding model/licence/security/resource benchmark, evaluation corpus/thresholds, plan budgets and organization-knowledge policy
- Milestone: M7

## 1. Objective
Define safe measurable advisory AI before adding Python/model dependencies.
## 2. Business Context
Generated tender requirements or claims are useful only when grounded, reviewable and authorized.
## 3. Scope
OpenAI generation routing/settings policy; local multilingual embedding adapter/pgvector policy; job/result/citation/budget schemas; retention and deletion; classified egress; multilingual tender terminology; natural uncertainty language; corpus and quality/cost thresholds; complete missing AI contracts before implementation.
## 4. Out of Scope
Model-selected decisions, autonomous approval/submission, arbitrary source scraping, procurement evaluation and public performance guarantees.
## 5. Technical Requirements
Job-scoped references, execution-time authority, structured unknown/unsupported output, exact citation versions, prompt/model/schema versions and human disposition. No shell/SQL/arbitrary URL/submit/award tools.
## 6. Proposed Implementation Approach
Select approved providers/legal terms; benchmark representative synthetic/authorized corpus; define thresholds and data minimization; approve schema/contracts and negative fixtures.
## 7. Dependencies
[Future scope](../architecture/BID-ARC-010.md), [provider policy](../architecture/BID-ARC-008.md).
## 8. Acceptance Criteria
- Given confidential input, then policy explicitly permits or denies each provider/region.
- Given unsupported material claim, then output schema labels it rather than inventing a citation.
- Given model/prompt update, then required regression gate is defined.
## 9. Testing Requirements
Corpus provenance, recall/citation/cost metric definitions and prompt-injection/tenant-leak scenario review.
## 10. Definition of Done
Approved AI contract/data/evaluation packet and privacy sign-off; no runtime capability claimed.

## 12. Risks and Notes
Source numerical targets remain proposals until corpus and acceptance thresholds are approved. Friendly language cannot replace exact internal state, source provenance or visible uncertainty.
